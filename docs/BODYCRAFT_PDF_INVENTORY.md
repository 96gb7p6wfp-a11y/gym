## Matching boundaries

- **Machine is not barbell:** PDF 6 and 9 depict BODYCRAFT lever presses. They cannot illustrate `bench-press`, `incline-bench-press` or `incline-db`.
- **Standing versus seated:** PDF 25 is the candidate for standing single-arm `lateral-raise`; PDF 23 depicts a seated bilateral variation. PDF 16–18 are supported seated fly variations, while current `cable-fly` instructions specify standing split stance.
- **Rows:** PDF 33 and 37 have no chest support; PDF 34 is the supported lever-machine candidate, subject to machine selection. PDF 35 uses cable resistance and free-hand support, not a dumbbell.
- **Pulldown:** PDF 32 depicts a wide overhand lat bar. `standard-lat-pulldown` and neutral-grip `lat-pulldown` remain distinct.
- **Arms:** PDF 44 uses a straight bar, not rope. PDF 45–46 do not establish the current overhead rope extension. PDF 41 is a cable curl despite the title Standing Bar Curl; it is not a free barbell curl.
- **Trunk:** PDF 49 is seated with a shoulder strap, not the current kneeling rope crunch. PDF 50 is seated, not floor reverse crunch or hanging knee raise. PDF 51 rotates the trunk; Pallof work resists rotation. PDF 52 bends laterally; suitcase work resists lateral bending.
- **Legs:** PDF 56 is the Elite model seated curl; the broad legacy `leg-curl` entry also accepts lying variants, so the image cannot be assigned by ID alone. PDF 57 is a seated attachment, not a 45-degree commercial sled. PDF 58 is a leg-press ankle press with near-straight knees, not standing calf raise or bent-knee seated calf raise.
- **Sports:** Cable golf/tennis/pitching plates are not medicine-ball throws, slams or volleyball approach jumps. Generic muscle similarity does not justify an image assignment.
