# Premium Home and Training: implementation references

Reviewed 10 November 2026. These are public first-party product and educational
references, not a hands-on comparison of the installed apps. Setline keeps its
own visual identity, local-first architecture and V9 prescriptions. No competitor
artwork, screenshots, exercise photographs or videos were copied into the app.

## Product principles applied

| Product and reviewed source | What the source establishes | Setline design decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hevy: exercise performance](https://www.hevyapp.com/features/exercise-performance/) | Exercise-specific history, charts, records for fixed repetition counts, and metrics suited to weighted, bodyweight, timed or cardio movements; instructions remain accessible during logging. | Put a supported comparison on Today and leave the full history in Progress. Compare like-for-like performances and preserve exercise-specific units. |
| [Strong: Exercise Detail](https://help.strongapp.io/article/237-about-exercise-detail) and [Profile Widgets](https://help.strongapp.io/article/239-profile-widgets) | Separate instructions/history/charts/records, best performances by rep count, relevant exercise/measurement widgets, and workouts-per-week consistency. These public help pages report February 2021 update dates. | Use a small number of personally relevant progress highlights and a compact actual-attendance summary; keep detailed technique behind the movement name. |
| [Gentler Streak: daily workout recommendations](https://docs.gentler.app/daily-workout-recommendations/how-to-find-and-understand-your-daily-workout-recommendation) | A concise main-screen recommendation leads to a detailed view where intensity and duration are understandable. | Keep the actual session dominant, one useful next action nearby, and adjustments inspectable. Do not import a physiological readiness score that Setline cannot measure. |
| [Apple: Fitness Summary guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph4c34a8a95/ios) and [official Fitness customization announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/watchos-11-brings-powerful-health-and-fitness-insights/) | The official indexed guide describes personalizable metrics, session history and trends; the opened Apple announcement confirms a configurable Summary and flexible activity goals/rest periods. | Prioritize useful summary information and a clear route to detail. Recorded consistency should motivate without penalizing rest or inventing streaks. |
| [Oura: How to Use the Oura App](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058599753-How-to-Use-the-Oura-App) | The page reports an October 7, 2026 update. Today emphasizes timely information and action items; historical detail is separate, and insufficient data is explicitly identified. | Show relevant available data, omit empty metric tiles, and offer an honest next action when a comparison is unavailable. Setline does not imitate Oura's sensor-based medical or readiness inferences. |
| [Fitbod: tracking volume, intensity and recovery](https://fitbod.me/blog/tracking-volume-intensity-and-recovery-with-fitbod/) | Logged sets, reps, load and effort provide recommendation context; manual changes are feedback. Duration alone does not describe workout difficulty. | Preserve deliberate entries and completed history, label logged load-volume accurately, and treat time as context rather than proof of productive training. |

The design decisions in the final column are Setline's interpretation of the
reviewed interaction principles, not claims that these products use Setline's
comparison algorithms or achievement definitions. No proprietary layout was
replicated. The pre-workout check is the user's requested interaction, not an
Oura/Gentler readiness-model implementation.

## Exercise identity and direction checks

| Reviewed educational source | Precise distinction supported |
| --- | --- |
| [ACE: Overhead Slams](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/182/overhead-slams/) | Two hands bring the ball overhead, then direct it downward into the floor. Suitable ball/floor clearance and rebound behavior matter. This is not a one-handed volleyball spike or a forward throw. |
| [ACE: Overhead Medicine Ball Throws](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/178/overhead-medicine-ball-throws/) | The ball travels forward from overhead toward a prepared partner or suitable wall. It has a different release direction and target from a floor slam or backward toss. |
| [UKSCA: jump-test reliability study](https://uksca.org.uk/blog/75/test-retest-reliability-of-different-jump-tests-using-the-output-sports-movement-sensor) | Its reactive drop-jump method steps off the box, contacts the ground with both feet, then rebounds vertically. The study's 30 cm box and hands-on-hips protocol are measurement conditions, not a reason to change V9's low-box dose or arm instructions. |
| [ACE: Landmine Training](https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/september-2025/8948/the-ace-workout-builder-for-landmine-training/) | One bar end is secured at a pivot and the free end moves through an arc. The article distinguishes press variations; it does not establish that every landmine press is a vertical dumbbell press or a push press. |

These references inform original concise wording and exact variant distinctions.
They do not replace individually stored impact/heavy-lifting guidance, provide
medical clearance, or authorize a prescription change. Any new diagrams are
original instructional schematics; source photographs and videos are not assets
licensed to Setline simply because the source is publicly readable.

## Access and verification boundaries

- Hevy, Strong, Gentler Streak, Oura, Fitbod, the Apple announcement and the
  listed ACE/UKSCA articles were opened and their relevant text inspected.
- Apple's current official Fitness guide was available through the search tool's
  indexed official content; direct guide fetches returned HTTP 429. The readable
  Apple announcement is older (June 2024), so it is not presented as proof of
  every detail of the newest installed Fitness release.
- [NSCA's landmine press article](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/nsca-coach/the-landmine-pressimplementation-and-variation/)
  exposed its abstract and a membership gate. Its full technique article/video
  was not reviewed and is not claimed as a verified demonstration.
- A general demonstration-search link remains a search link. Neither a search
  result nor an unplayed external video is labeled as a reviewed demonstration.
- No physical-iPhone behavior, competitor app performance or sensor accuracy was
  validated by reading these pages. Setline's functional and visual test results
  belong in its separate release verification record.
