## Publication checklist for a future approved photo

1. Confirm the canonical ID plus selected variation, equipment, attachment, grip, side and movement/hold direction. A shared muscle group is insufficient.
2. Record original source URL/file, photographer/rightsholder, written commercial copying/adaptation/rehosting/offline-distribution terms, model/property releases and required attribution. Keep written evidence, not an assumption based on public access.
3. Review start/finish positions and any arrows/caption with a qualified coach. Do not crop hands, feet, equipment or safe landing/throwing space.
4. Make optimized WebP/AVIF derivatives and a fallback with explicit dimensions/aspect ratio, concise alt text and graceful offline/error behavior. Do not embed full PDF page screenshots.
5. Record the approval and exact verification in the media manifest before enabling the asset. Unapproved/incorrect media must remain unavailable without blocking text guides or logging.
