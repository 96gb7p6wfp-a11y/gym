# Setline: quieter, more satisfying training

Review: 22 October 2026. This is a refinement of Setline Soft, not another visual
reset. Existing data calculations, navigation, prescriptions and actions remain.

## Public pattern review

The official Apple Fitness+, Hevy and Strong pages returned HTTP200 and their text
was inspected during this task:

- https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/ — a clear session identity, personal
  plan and immediately legible real-time feedback. Apply this by giving today's
  training title and primary action a deliberate focal hierarchy.
- https://www.hevyapp.com/ — simple workout logging, routines and progress are
  central. Apply this by keeping the current set controls stationary and making
  completed sets visibly acknowledged without introducing a separate screen.
- https://www.strong.app/ — “Think less. Lift more.” and a notebook that stays out
  of the way. Apply this by keeping feedback inline and preserving direct actions.

Gentler Streak (gentler.app), Oura (ouraring.com) and WHOOP (whoop.com) were denied
by the configured proxy (tunnel403). They were not live-inspected in this task.
The earlier repository research records additional official screenshots already
reviewed. This task's new review was page text, not a hands-on native-app test.
No assets, branding or layouts are copied.

## Concrete refinements

- Today uses a matte Morandi wash, stronger primary-action typography, and a brief
  title transition on a date/workout change. The content and geometry stay quiet;
  there is no new dashboard, statistic, badge collection or permanent navigation.
- A logged set receives a drawn check in the existing coach line. Completed set
  circles become softly filled. Inputs and completion controls remain available;
  nothing overlays the workout or requires dismissal.
- Workout completion emphasizes actual recorded minutes, completed sets and
  genuine personal records. Real records receive the accent; there are no invented
  improvements, celebratory numbers or confetti. Recovery guidance becomes a short
  editorial note rather than another card.
- Progress changes one metric at a time with a short transition and restrained
  chart-line/bar motion. The empty jump state now explains what to record. Actual
  recorded trends, measured versus estimated distinctions and insight logic remain.

All animation is brief and excluded for reduced-motion preferences. Existing
light/dark semantic tokens,44px controls, safe areas and local assets are retained.
Final browser screenshots and relevant workflow checks establish layout quality;
physical-iPhone audio verification remains separately required.
